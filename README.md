# JobScout Lite

**Copyright © 2026 Noumaan Nabi. All rights reserved.**

A free job-search dashboard that pulls listings from a wide range of
company career pages and job boards, scores each one for how
trustworthy it looks, and gives you the tools to actually manage a job
search in one place — instead of juggling a dozen browser tabs and a
spreadsheet.

**Live:** [job-scout-lite-gamma.vercel.app](https://job-scout-lite-gamma.vercel.app)

## What it does

- **Aggregated listings.** Jobs and internships pulled from a wide mix
  of company career pages and job boards — remote-friendly roles from
  around the world alongside India-based openings — refreshed every 6
  hours, so what you're looking at is current, not a stale cache from
  last week.
- **Trust scoring.** Every listing gets a trust indicator, so you can
  tell at a glance which postings look legitimate and which look
  sketchy, instead of finding out the hard way three interview rounds
  in.
- **Resume Intelligence.** Upload your resume and get a plain-English
  breakdown: formatting issues that trip up automated screening
  systems, a skills read, and an overall score with concrete
  suggestions — plus a shortlist of currently-listed jobs that
  actually match what's on it.
- **Career Roadmap.** Structured, topic-by-topic learning paths for
  Frontend, Backend, Data & ML, DevOps & Cloud, and Mobile roles —
  pick a track and see exactly what to learn next, with progress you
  can track as you go.
- **Application tracker.** Save jobs, log applications, and move them
  through stages on a kanban board or a table — whichever you'd
  rather look at — plus an interview tracker so upcoming rounds don't
  slip through the cracks.
- **Free tools & resources.** A curated set of free sites and tools
  for the parts of a job search that fall outside "browse listings" —
  resume builders, prep material, and a few underrated finds.

## Why it's useful

- **One place, not ten tabs.** Listings, resume feedback, a learning
  plan, and an application tracker, without bouncing between
  unrelated tools that don't talk to each other.
- **Less time filtering junk.** The trust scoring exists specifically
  to cut down on time spent on listings that turn out to be
  low-quality or outright fake.
- **Free.** No paid tier, no ads, no account limits. Built as a
  personal project to solve a real problem, not as a product looking
  for a monetization angle.

## A couple of honest notes

- This is a solo project, built and maintained by one person, not a
  company or a team. Expect it to keep evolving.
- Trust scores are a signal, not a guarantee — they're meant to help
  you prioritize where to look closer, not to replace your own
  judgment before applying anywhere or handing over personal
  information.
- Listings come from public, third-party sources. JobScout Lite
  doesn't control what companies post — always verify a listing
  independently (the company's own site is a good first check) before
  applying, especially for anything that asks for payment or sensitive
  information upfront.
